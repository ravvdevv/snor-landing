What it does

Everything on-device. Anything off-device, on your terms.

Local inference

Download and run GGUF models on your phone with GPU acceleration via Vulkan. No internet required after download.

Cloud fallback

Seamlessly switch to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, or Kimi when you need more power. You choose where inference runs.

Multimodal chat

Send text and images in conversations. Vision works with local models (Qwen2-VL) and cloud providers alike.

Persistent sessions

Chats, tasks, and settings live in local storage via Hive. Nothing leaves your device unless you explicitly pick cloud mode.

Smart auto-config

On first launch, Snor reads your device’s RAM and sets optimal context size and token limits automatically.

Task workflows

A dedicated task view for structured AI-assisted workflows, alongside free-form chat.

Privacy

Your data has a boundary: your device.

Local inference runs entirely on-device. Chats are stored locally, models are local, and nothing is sent anywhere without an explicit choice.

Even in cloud mode, API keys are kept in local storage and transmitted only to the provider’s endpoint — never to Snor, never to a third party.
