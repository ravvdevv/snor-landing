# Snor

**Offline AI chat and local LLMs for Android.**

Run supported GGUF and LiteRT-LM language models on your phone with local-first privacy. No account is required, local inference works without internet after downloads, and cloud AI is opt-in.
</div>

## Download

* **Android APK (v1.0.8, ARM64 recommended):** https://github.com/ravvdevv/snor-landing/releases/latest/download/snor-v1.0.8.apk
* **Other ABIs:** [armeabi-v7a](https://github.com/ravvdevv/snor-landing/releases/latest/download/app-armeabi-v7a-release.apk) (~36.0 MB) · [x86_64](https://github.com/ravvdevv/snor-landing/releases/latest/download/app-x86_64-release.apk) (~48.2 MB)
* Pick the APK matching your device ABI on any **Android 8.0+** device.
* Enable **"Install from unknown sources"** when prompted.
* **Live site:** https://www.snor.site/
